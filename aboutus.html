Our Story

1990

The Beginning

Our story began in 1990, when our founder, Hector, started working alongside Mike Kuhar at California's Best Refinishing Company. Beginning as a helper, Hector quickly developed a passion for the craft. He spent those early years learning new painting techniques, experimenting with finishes and paint mixtures, and building the foundation for what would become a lifelong career in painting.

1998

Early Growth

By 1998, Hector began taking on small projects of his own. His first independent job was painting a garage door on Balboa Island, referred to him by his former boss, Mike Kuhar. While continuing to work full time, Hector used his days off to complete small painting projects and further develop his skills. During this period, he also worked at Pacific Bluffs Industrial Park, where he handled maintenance and painting responsibilites. His growing experience eventually led him to obtain his first California handyman license in Costa Mesa.

2001

Expanding His Craft

In 2001, after more than a decade of learning and hands on experience, Hector joined Local Brush, one of Southern California's respected painting companies at the time. There he continued refining his craft, gaining extensive experience with water-based and oil-based coatings, material application, surface preparation, and a variety of brush and roller techniques. These years helped shape the attention to detail and commitment to quality that remain central to the company today.

2005

Leon's Painting Is Founded

In 2005, Hector took the next major step in his career and founded Leon's Painting. What began with small independent projects had grown into a business built around a simple goal: provide customers with dependable service, exceptional craftsmanship, and finishes that stand the test of time.

2007

Becoming a Licensed Contractor

In 2007, Hector earned his California State License Board contractor license, officially beginning a new chapter as a licensed painting contractor. This milestone allowed the company to continue growing while taking on larger and more specialized projects, all while maintaining the same standards of quality and personal attention that had defined Hector's work from the beginning.

Expanding Our Capabilities

That same year, and opportunity arose to aquire a dedicated shop equipped with professional spray booths. The new facility opened the door to more specialized finishing work and allowed the company to achieve a higher level of consistency, precision, and craftsmanship. From detailed wood finishes to larger painting projects, the shop became an important part of the company's ability to deliver high-quality results.

Present

Looking Ahead

Today, we are proud to continue that journey under a new name and a renewed vision. As Hector L.F, we carry forward more than three decades of experience, craftsmanship, and dedication to our customers. While the name may have changed, the values behind the work remain the same: quality, integrity, attention to detail, and pride in every project. We are grateful to the cusotmers, families, businesses, and communities that have trusted us throughout the years. Every project has played a part in our story, and we look forward to continuing that tradition for many years to come. Thank you for being part of our journey.
