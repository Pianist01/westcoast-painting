Our Story

Where It Started

The roots of HLF Painting & Finishing begin with a family tradition of crafstmanship. Long before HLF was established, Hector Leon Decion built decades of experience in the painting and finishing industry. Beginning his career in Southern California, he developed his craft through years of hands on work, learning techniques and standards that he would eventually pass on to the next generation.

Learning the Trade

For Hector(founder of HLF Painting & Finishing), that next generation began at just 16 years old. Working alongside his father, Hector Leon Decion, he was introduced to the painting industry from the ground up. He learned that quality work goes far beyond applying paint. It begins with proper preparation, attention to detail, understanding materials, and taking pride in the finished product. Years spent working beside his father provided hands on experience while also teaching him the work ethic and crafstmanship required to build a reputation customers can trust.

Carrying the Craft Forward

As his experience grew, so did his ability to take on increasingly demanding painting and finishing work. The techniques passed down through his father became the foundation for developing his own approach to the trade. Rather than simply continuing someone else's business, he began building toward something of his own, combining the knowledge he inherited with his own standards, experience, and vision for serving clients.

HLF Painting & Finishing

HLF Painting & Finishing represents the next chapter of that journey. Built on years of hands on experience beginning at 16, HLF carries forward a family tradition of crafstmanship while establishing an identity of its own. Today, HLF Painting & Finishing is committed to delivering dependable service, careful preparation, attention to detail, and high-quality finishes on every project. Every job is an opportunity to demonstrate the skills developed over years in the trade and the values passed from one generation to the next.
